## Bounded workflow proposal

Students sign in to view and submit their linked enrollment checklist. Enrollment staff review submitted requirements, approve them or return them with a correction note. Students correct and resubmit returned items. Approved status is reflected in the checklist completion count. The Week 15 work adds traceable tests, observed defect fixes, regression evidence, reliability/error handling and security hardening using Flutter Web, Laravel, Sanctum and SQLite.

## Users and controlled data

The implemented actors are students and enrollment staff. Records and credentials are fictional and local-only. No production school integration or real personal records are authorized by this form. Scanned-document uploads, reminders and production deployment are outside the current bounded slice.
